I am a research scientist at [Meta Reality Labs](https://about.meta.com/realitylabs/), where I lead sensing research for wearable devices. I am interested in how sensing can help personal devices understand our movements, intentions, and everyday context, enabling more natural interactions with technology.

I am also an Associate Professor on leave at the [National University of Singapore](https://nus.edu.sg/), where my research focuses on wireless bioelectronic devices. I received my PhD in electrical engineering from Stanford University. My work has been recognized by Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia and MIT Technology Review's Innovators Under 35 Asia, and I serve on the editorial board of _Physical Review Applied_.

Implantable photonic devices for wireless light delivery deep into living systems.
Applications include control of neural activity using optogenetics, and selective
activation of light-sensitive cancer drugs for photodynamic therapy. These systems combine
the spatiotemporal control of bioelectronics with the molecular selectivity of light for
enhanced therapeutic precision.

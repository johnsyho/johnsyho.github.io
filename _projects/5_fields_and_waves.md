Fundamental research on fields and waves motivated by practical challenges in biomedical
technology. Areas of interest include metasurfaces, propagation in complex media,
electromagnetic chirality, spoof plasmonics, and parity-time symmetry, with emphasis on
their applications to wireless power transfer, communication, actuation, and sensing.

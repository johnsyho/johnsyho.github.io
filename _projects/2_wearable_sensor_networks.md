Networks of wearable sensors that continuously monitor health over the whole body. The
approach uses electromagnetically functional clothing to establish wireless connectivity
between multiple distributed battery-free sensors, with the aim of creating full-body
physiological maps of electrical, mechanical, thermal, and chemical activity.
